New Health The movement

The manifesto

A new generation of startups building tomorrow's health

We are digital health startups that help people with everyday health problems, and stay with them over time. We are joining forces so the world knows this kind of care exists, and to give it the place it deserves in the future of health.

We don't ask “how many more years?” We ask “how are you today?”

Today, health is caught between two extremes. On one side, medicine that waits until you are really ill before it steps in. On the other, longevity, which promises extra years to people who are already well and want to push their limits.

In between are tens of millions of people no one is looking after. Their everyday life is not what it used to be. They sleep badly. They put on weight without understanding why. They have to cope with the ups and downs of their digestion, their skin, their energy, their hormones, their mood. They just want to feel like themselves again. Yet for them, proper care, the kind that stays with you over time, remains out of reach.

They were told it was nothing, that it was their age, or stress, or all in their head. They were advised to eat better, move more, manage their stress. They tried, and they are back where they started. Their journey is long and disheartening, and it ends with the words that shut the door: “you'll have to learn to live with it.” We don't accept that answer.

If answers have been missing until now, it's because these problems fall between specialities, below what standard tests can pick up, and through the cracks of appointments that are too short. Joining the dots, and staying with someone over time, took tools that didn't exist yet.

Those tools now exist: at-home measurement, remote follow-up, scientific literature you can search in real time. What was missing was technology put to work in care designed around these people. It is here now.

So a generation of startups is rising, digital by nature and specialised, each one tackling specific issues. There was New Space, when private companies moved into a sector that governments had run alone. There was New Food, when we rethought what we put on our plates. Now comes New Health.

Our commitments

Here is what we commit to.

1

Seeing you as an individual

  • We start with your body, not an average.
  • We take the time to explain.
  • We look for the science that applies to you.
2

Staying with you over time

  • We build a plan, not a list of tips.
  • We revise it when your situation changes.
  • We're there every step of the way.
3

Making expertise accessible

  • We come to you, wherever you live.
  • We don't make you wait for months.
  • We make sure it fits into everyday life.

We don't replace your doctor. We work with doctors, and we hand back to them as soon as they need to step in.

Feeling supported along the way shouldn't be a privilege for people who are already healthy, or something that only starts the day you fall ill. For the first time, this kind of care can reach you at home, wherever you live, even in the most remote regions.

New Health isn't a brand. It's a direction we share. We don't promise miracles, or that you will live longer. We promise to be there for you, and to help you find ways to live better.

You were told to learn to live with it. We refuse to accept that.

To the companies building this kind of care: join us.
