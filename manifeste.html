The manifesto

A new generation of startups that won't promise you a longer life

We are digital health startups that support people over time with everyday health issues. We are coming together so the world knows this kind of health exists, and to give it its rightful place in the health of tomorrow.

We don't ask “how many more years?” We ask “how are you today?”

Today, health is caught between two extremes. On one side, medicine that waits until you are really ill before it steps in. On the other, longevity, which promises extra years to people who are already well and want to push their limits.

In between are tens of millions of people no one is looking after. Their everyday life is not what it used to be. They sleep badly. They put on weight without understanding why. They have to cope with the ups and downs of their digestion, their skin, their fatigue, their hormones, their mood. They simply want to feel at ease in their daily lives again. Yet for them, real care, with follow-up over time, remains out of reach.

They were told it was nothing, that it was their age, or stress, or all in their head. They were advised to eat better, move more, manage their stress. They tried, and they are back where they started. Their journey is long and disheartening, and it ends with a sentence that closes the door: “you'll have to learn to live with it.” We refuse that sentence.

If answers have been missing until now, it is because these issues slip between specialties, below the threshold of standard tests, between two appointments that are too short. Connecting them and supporting someone over time required tools that did not exist yet.

Those tools now exist: at-home measurement, remote follow-up, scientific literature you can search in real time. What was missing was technology serving a pathway designed for these people. It is here now.

So a generation of startups is rising, digital by nature and specialised, each one tackling specific issues. There was New Space, when private companies took over a space sector that governments had run alone. There was New Food, when we rethought what we put on our plates. Now comes New Health.

Our commitments

Here is what we commit to.

1

Seeing you as an individual

  • We start from your body, not from an average.
  • We take the time to explain.
  • We look for the science that applies to you.
2

Supporting you over time

  • We build a pathway, not a list of tips.
  • We revise it when your situation changes.
  • We stay reachable all the way through.
3

Making expertise accessible

  • We come to you, wherever you live.
  • We don't make you wait for months.
  • We make sure it fits into everyday life.

We do not take your doctor's place. We work with doctors, and we hand things back to them as soon as it is their turn to act.

Feeling supported along the way should not be a privilege, reserved for those who are already well or put on hold until the day you fall ill. For the first time, this level of follow-up can reach you at home, wherever you live, even in the most remote regions.

New Health is not a brand, it is a shared direction. We don't promise miracles, or that you will live longer. We promise to be there for you, and to help you find ways to live better.

You were told to learn to live with it. We refuse to accept that.

To the companies building this kind of health: join us.
