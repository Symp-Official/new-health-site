Asked at the counter

Questions.

Is it free?

Yes. Because it's free, we read every request one by one.

Does joining the waitlist guarantee me a spot?

No. It means you get the programme and the registration link first.

Can I help?

Please do. Pick something on the to-do list, or tell us what we missed.

Do I have to stay all day?

No. Come for the talks you care about.

Is this a longevity event?

No. It's about how companies that help people feel better day to day get built, funded and trusted.

Why Brussels?

It's the capital of Europe, less than two hours by train from Paris, Amsterdam and Cologne.

Who's behind it?

The team behind New Health, the movement of everyday-health startups, hosted by WAT in its new garage. And anyone who helps us build it.
